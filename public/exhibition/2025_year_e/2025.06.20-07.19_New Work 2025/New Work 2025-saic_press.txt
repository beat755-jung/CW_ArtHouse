Hyeyoon Jung depicts dreamlike, distorted images of birds to channel subconscious emotions. Despite her stated aversion to birds, Jung returns to them obsessively, crafting forms that flutter between self-portrait and symbols of nature. Drawing inspiration from art history, Jung reinterprets iconic visual languages to express her inspirations: The Studio uses the color palette of Philip Guston’s painting of the same name, Endless Pecking echoes the surrealist tone of Max Ernst, and Right Person, Wrong Location is inspired by M.C. Escher’s use of ambiguous space. Across these works, Jung uses historical styles as frameworks to explore her own fragmented sense of belonging.
