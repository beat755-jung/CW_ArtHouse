<The world is my oyster?> - Air-dry clay, clear nail polish, double sided tape, usb light, 2024
By @hyeyoonlillyjung

From the Shakespeare's play 'The Merry Wives of Windsor' published in 1602, there is a quote saying “Why, then, the world's mine oyster, Which I with sword will open.” From this play, a famous phasing "The world is your oyster" comes out. However, I question if the world really is so in this period of time. In this period of time where there are so many things going on for this generation to think and consider of, they do not have time to create their world, their oyster to taste.

(Ideally with 35 clay cones in a site-specific space)
