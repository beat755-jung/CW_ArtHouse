<Right Person, Wrong Location> - 2025, Steel, ¼" plywood, gesso, acrylic paint, wood glue, hot glue, soil, dust, 38 x 33 x 13”

By @hyeyoonlillyjung

Right Person, Wrong Location reflects a connection that felt destined, yet dissolved quietly under the misalignment of time and physical distance. Once painless became strained when values and rhythm of words shifted from one another, becoming unfamiliar.

Metal wings represent sincere emotions and a desire to reach the person one longs to be close to, but they are too heavy to fly. Though the feelings were genuine, they remained grounded and unfulfilled for one or both sides.

It wasn’t the wrong person, just misaligned timing and place. Even faded connections hold meaning, for they were once true. The right person, just wrong location and timing.
